The idea
One Coin. One Collection.
$QUANTS is the platform coin and Quants are its NFTs. Anyone can also launch a hybrid NFT token: a token that comes with its own NFT collection, swappable the same way.
Why? Built on the Metaplex 404 standard, an NFT swaps for its coin at a fixed rate, any time. That gives NFTs a liquid market without waiting for a willing buyer or a willing seller.

How it works
One Quant.
A Fixed 0 $QUANTS.
There is a set number of Quant NFTs, and each one swaps for the same fixed amount of $QUANTS.
Step 1
Buy $QUANTS
Buy at least 100k $QUANTS, the platform coin, on the open market.
Step 2
Get A Quant
20% of the total NFTs go straight into the pool. The rest you can mint with 100,000 tokens + 0.5 SOL. Minted quants start at Mid-Level; quants taken from the pool start as Juniors.
Step 3
Earn Rewards
Your quant earns stock every workday.
Step 4
Move Up
Promote your quant. The higher its rank, the more it earns.
